Engaging Our Communities

We are deeply committed to strengthening the communities in which we operate through fundraising, volunteerism, and partnerships that advance our Judgement Free™ values. Throughout the year, we help advance wellbeing and create a positive impact where our members and team members live, work, and work out.

Judgement Free Generation®

The Judgement Free Generation® (JFG) is our signature philanthropic initiative designed to advance youth wellbeing and empower the next generation to create kinder communities in partnership with Boys & Girls Clubs of America (BGCA). Click below to learn more about our 10+ year partnership with BGCA and how we are providing young people with opportunities to feel supported, valued, and confident.

Managing Our Environmental Impact

Our commitment to environmental stewardship is founded on the belief that mitigating the impacts of our business on the planet is crucial for sustaining both our long-term growth and the resilience and wellbeing of our members, team members, franchisees, and communities. Our environmental management approach focuses on sustainable practices that enhance operational efficiency, reduce our footprint, and drive progress across our value chain.
